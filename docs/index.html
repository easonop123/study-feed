The bit that actually moves your grade

It marks what you write — then shows you how to write it better.

Write a real answer and it gets marked against Achieved, Merit and Excellence. Most apps stop at telling you what's missing. This one quotes your own sentences back and shows you the version that would have earned the higher grade.

Achieved
Name the mechanism, not just the outcome “the reaction goes faster” The reaction goes faster because the particles gain kinetic energy and collide more frequently.
Link collision energy to activation energy “they bump into each other more” More of those collisions now exceed the activation energy, so a greater proportion are successful.
Next time: for every “it increases”, add the “because” that names the particle behaviour.

A tutor sits in the corner of every screen and it can already see the card you're on — so “why is that the answer?” just works. Stuck on something in a textbook? Photograph it and ask about the photo.

Why is that the answer?
Because the rate depends on how often particles collide with enough energy. Grinding the solid doesn't change the energy — it changes how many particles are exposed to collide in the first place.
